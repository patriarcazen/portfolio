Case Study 01 HR Operations & People Systems

Distributed HRIS & Workforce Automation Engine

Chargeflow, Inc. · Supporting 80+ Remote Team Members across US & APAC

Role & Scope System Architect & Operations Lead

The Operational Challenge

A high-velocity distributed contractor workforce relied on fragmented spreadsheets, isolated messaging threads, and manual time sheets. With no unified system of record, attendance and shift scheduling caused frequent confusion, overtime and payroll calculations took 3+ business days to audit, and routine requests created major administrative bottlenecks for HR.

The Operational Solution

Architected and deployed a centralized cloud-based HRIS integrating time tracking, leave management, weekly shift scheduling, structured approvals, and executive payroll reporting. Designed role-based permissions, automated approval routing, and self-service dashboards to eliminate HR dependency and ensure audit-ready compliance.

Case Study 02 Global Talent Acquisition

High-Velocity Global Recruiting Engine & Candidate Experience

55 KNOTS & Bluestone Servicing · Scaling Creative & Engineering Headcount

Role & Scope Talent Acquisition Lead

The Sourcing & Hiring Challenge

The agency and technical teams faced rapid client growth requiring immediate headcount expansion across specialized creative roles (multimedia designers, UI/UX creatives) and technical talent (QA leads, developers). Sourcing was ad-hoc, interview loops were unstructured, and prolonged hiring cycles caused candidate drop-off in a competitive market.

The Structured Sourcing Solution

Built ongoing passive talent pipelines via targeted multi-channel sourcing, standardized ATS interview stages (Ashby/Greenhouse), and introduced objective role-based scorecards to align hiring managers. Implemented a rigorous 48-hour feedback SLA to maintain positive candidate engagement from initial reachout to written offer.
